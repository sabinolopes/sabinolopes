## About me

I'm a front-end developer with 2+ years of experience at [Start by WGSN](https://startbywgsn.com/), a content and trend platform for the fashion industry. Our front-end runs on a proprietary, framework-free architecture (object-oriented components and JSX rendered through an in-house Hyperscript), so I work close to the DOM, with a strong focus on performance and clean component design.

I come from a full stack background (Trybe, 1,500 hours), take on back-end tasks when needed, and recently started working on our mobile app with Flutter and Dart.

## What I've been building

- 🤖 **Generative AI tools for fashion**: garment creation from sketches, inspiration images or photos, plus a virtual try-on
- ✍️ **Content editor** built on Editor.js, with image grids and color palettes, used daily by our editorial team
- 🕓 **Version history** for content editors, with automatic Firebase backups before saving
- 🔎 **Technical SEO** with schema.org structured data across the platform
- 🎓 **[Lessons Platform](https://github.com/LuizHenriqueBrazFerreira/lesson-plataform)**: e-learning platform built as a volunteer with React, Node.js, Express, MySQL and Docker

Most of my professional work lives in private repositories, but feel free to explore the public projects pinned below.
